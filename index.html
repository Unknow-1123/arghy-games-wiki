About this project

One place for everything about A.R.GH.Y games.

A.R.GH.Y Games Wiki is the official knowledge base for games developed by A.R.GH.Y.

Its purpose is to provide clear, accurate and useful information for players, from basic gameplay explanations to detailed walkthroughs and puzzle solutions.

The Wiki is maintained by the developer. Public visitors can browse its pages, while editing access remains restricted.

What you can find here

  • Detailed walkthroughs
  • Task and objective explanations
  • Puzzle solutions
  • Character and item information
  • Gameplay mechanics and controls
  • Technical support and troubleshooting
